GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers before you begin.

Can I try BOR AI without paying?

Yes. Free chat is available with usage limits. No payment is required to start as a guest.

Do I need a Google account?

No. Guest mode lets you try chat without signing in. Guest conversations are not saved across reloads. Sign in with Google to use account-based chat history.

Can I buy Basic or Pro today?

Not yet. The paid plans shown above are proposals. Live checkout, credit allocations, and paid entitlements are not available.

Should I rely on AI answers without checking?

No. AI can make mistakes. Review important facts and avoid submitting passwords, secrets, or other sensitive information.
