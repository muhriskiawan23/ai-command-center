# $BORAI — Tokenomics Research Framework

**Status:** Research only — no token design approved  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-01

## 1. Purpose

Determine whether a token is necessary for the BOR AI ecosystem and, only if justified, identify a safe, compliant, technically feasible design. The existence of a token must not be assumed to create product utility or business value.

## 2. Questions to answer before token design

1. What user problem requires a blockchain token rather than ordinary accounts, credits, subscriptions, or payments?
2. Who needs the token, and what specific action or benefit requires holding or using it?
3. Can the proposed utility be delivered without a token at lower cost and risk?
4. Which jurisdictions, licensing, marketing, tax, consumer-protection, and financial-promotion rules may apply?
5. What security, custody, smart-contract, treasury, governance, and incident-response responsibilities arise?
6. What are the recurring costs for deployment, monitoring, audits, liquidity, community operations, and compliance?

## 3. Research workstreams

- Utility and product dependency.
- Chain comparison: security, tooling, fees, ecosystem maturity, reliability, and operational burden.
- Supply and allocation scenario modelling, only after utility is established.
- Distribution, vesting, treasury, governance, and conflict-of-interest controls.
- Smart-contract threat model and independent audit requirements.
- Legal, tax, consumer-protection, and marketing review with qualified professionals.
- Transparent disclosure of risks and limits; no promises of returns or price appreciation.

## 4. Draft model template

No values are approved. Any future model must explicitly state assumptions and include:
- Maximum supply and mint authority.
- Initial and circulating supply.
- Founder/team, ecosystem, community, treasury, and other allocations if justified.
- Vesting and unlock schedules.
- Utility flows and demand drivers.
- Treasury controls and signer policy.
- Upgrade, pause, and emergency powers.
- Liquidity assumptions and ongoing obligations.
- Scenario analysis and sensitivity to low/zero demand.

## 5. Hard restrictions

Until Boris separately approves a go/no-go decision and the required legal/security work:
- Do not deploy contracts or mint tokens.
- Do not sell, presell, airdrop, distribute, or promote a live token.
- Do not create liquidity pools or seek exchange listings.
- Do not publish token supply/allocation figures as final.
- Do not ask for or store seed phrases, private keys, or wallet credentials.

## 6. Current conclusion

The token's necessity, chain, utility, supply, allocation, vesting, governance, and launch schedule are all undecided. The immediate priority is to build and validate the BOR AI product independently. A token should proceed only if it solves a demonstrated ecosystem need and passes legal, security, and economic review.
