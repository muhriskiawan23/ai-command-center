Refund Policy

This policy explains refund and cancellation arrangements for BOR AI subscriptions sold through Paddle. It applies alongside the terms shown during checkout and any rights that apply under law.

Current status: BOR AI paid plans are not available for purchase as of November 25, 2026. The Free plan does not require payment. No paid subscription or credit top-up is being sold through the app at this time.

1. Seller and payment support

When a BOR AI purchase is made through Paddle checkout, Paddle acts as the reseller and seller of record for the transaction, processes the payment, and provides first-line order support. Please use the support link in your Paddle receipt or buyer account for payment, invoice, and refund requests. You may also contact BOR AI at support@getborai.com for service-related help.

2. Refund requests

For eligible Paddle transactions, customers may request a refund within 14 days of a one-time purchase or within 14 days of the most recent subscription renewal. Paddle reviews refund requests and processes approved refunds to the original payment method, subject to its terms, payment method rules, and applicable law. A request does not guarantee approval. Where applicable law gives you a stronger or additional right, that right is not limited by this policy.

3. Subscription cancellation

If a paid subscription is offered, checkout and the Paddle buyer account will explain how to cancel. Unless checkout says otherwise, cancellation stops future renewals; access may continue until the end of the current paid period. Cancellation does not itself refund a previous charge. Refund requests are handled as described above.

4. Credits and service issues

Paid credit top-ups are not currently available. If they are introduced, the checkout will disclose whether credits expire, how they are used, and any product-specific refund conditions before purchase. If you paid but did not receive the service, contact Paddle using your receipt and contact BOR AI at support@getborai.com so we can investigate.

5. Policy updates

We may update this policy before paid checkout becomes available. Any material purchase-specific conditions will be shown before payment. Questions: support@getborai.com.
