Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how BOR AI handles personal information when you visit getborai.com or use app.getborai.com.

1. Who operates BOR AI

BOR AI is an independent project operated by Riskiawan (Boris), Indonesia (“BOR AI,” “we,” or “us”). For privacy questions or requests, contact support@getborai.com.

2. Information we process

  • Account information: when you sign in with Google, we receive the account details Google makes available, such as your name, email address, and account identifier.
  • Prompts and conversations: text and files you choose to submit, generated responses, and optional memory items you explicitly save. Signed-in chat history is stored so you can view and manage it. Guest conversations are temporary and are not saved as account history.
  • Service and security data: technical information needed to operate, protect, troubleshoot, and prevent abuse of the service.
  • Payment data: paid checkout is not currently active and BOR AI does not currently collect subscription payments through the app. If Paddle checkout is introduced, Paddle will process payment and transaction information as reseller/seller of record; BOR AI will receive information needed to manage access and support.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide chats and account features, maintain saved history, respond to support requests, protect the service, diagnose errors, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information. We do not use your private chat history for advertising.

4. Service providers and transfers

We rely on service providers to operate BOR AI, including Google for sign-in, Cloudflare for hosting and related infrastructure, and AI providers to generate responses. Information you submit may be sent to the relevant AI provider to answer your request. If payments are enabled, Paddle will process checkout and payment. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices and may handle it in countries outside your own.

5. Storage, retention, and deletion

We keep account and conversation information while needed to provide the service, maintain your history, protect the service, and meet legal obligations. You can delete saved conversations and optional memory through the app’s available controls. Guest conversations are held temporarily in the page session. Some limited records may remain in security backups or logs for a period needed for recovery, security, or legal compliance, then are deleted or de-identified under operational schedules.

6. Your choices and requests

You can choose whether to sign in, avoid submitting sensitive information, delete saved history and memory using the app controls, and contact us to ask about access, correction, deletion, or other privacy rights that apply to you. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

7. Security and children

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. BOR AI is not designed for children to use without appropriate adult supervision. Please do not submit another person’s sensitive information without a lawful basis.

8. Changes

We may update this Policy as the service changes. The updated date will appear at the top of this page. For privacy questions, email support@getborai.com.
